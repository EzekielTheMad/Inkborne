I.The sheet

Every detail has
a place at the table.

Ability scores, saving throws, equipment, and the choices that made this character yours. Keep the useful details together, ready for the next turn.

II.The story

Remember what
the dice started.

The keeper with a secret. The road you did not take. The promise your character still means to keep. Give those threads a home beside the sheet.

A notebook for the things a modifier cannot tell you.

III.Your table, your ideas

Leave a little room
in the margins.

A spell from the fen. A feat earned on the road. A background that belongs to your world. Create spells, feats, and backgrounds in your homebrew library.

Make something your table will remember.

IV · Written in the open

A place for your story.
A say in what comes next.

Inkborne is open source. A character tool should invite the same curiosity and creativity as the games it serves: room to understand it, question it, and help it grow.
